## Acceptance checks for every subsequent replacement

Trace each arm shoulder → elbow → wrist → hand and each leg hip → knee → ankle → foot. Check consistent segment lengths between phases, joint direction, limb count, equipment contact, weight-bearing support, camera continuity and the named movement. Inspect at full size before saving. Failed candidates must remain outside the app/drop-here replacement path.
