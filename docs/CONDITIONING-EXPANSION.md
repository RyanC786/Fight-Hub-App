# Conditioning and exercise guidance — 27 September 2026

## Implemented

- 82 exercises with movement-specific steps, form cues and easier options.
- 25, 30 and 40 minute conditioning sessions. All include 6 minutes of dynamic preparation and 5 minutes of recovery; the interval blocks are respectively 14, 19 and 29 minutes. These are total elapsed targets, not continuous high-intensity work.
- Home, military-inspired and quiet/no-jumping circuits, with eight movements per rotation. Starter uses 20/40 work/rest, Build 30/30, Hard 40/20. Starter does not select advanced or jumping movements. Longer sessions end with a partial rotation when necessary.
- Pause/resume, automatic pause on hidden page or leaving the timer, early cooldown, and explicit editable logging after the timer. Elapsed time never marks exercises complete.
- Warm-up and cooldown guides around both custom and guided workouts. These guides are untimed and do not claim to verify completion.
- Example sets/repetitions/rest by movement type, user-selected workload examples, and actual load logging in kilograms per weight. These are original editorial examples, not personalised or professionally reviewed prescriptions.
- 81 realistic AI-generated illustrations, enlarged on request. The seated leg-curl image is withheld after three variants showed misleading roller placement. All 82 entries have written instructions. Visual inspection is not professional technique approval.

## Editorial basis and boundaries

[NHS warm-up guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/how-to-warm-up-before-exercising/) recommends at least six minutes and gradual preparation. Our routine uses marching, heel taps, shoulder movement and shallow knee bends. Warm-up time can be extended; static holds are placed in the cooldown.

[NHS post-exercise stretching](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/how-to-stretch-after-exercising/) describes a roughly five-minute gentle routine and 15–20 second holds. Our original text and images accompany balanced-side stretch slots; these slots include transitions and relaxation, not a continuous 60-second hold.

[ACSM's 2026 resistance-training update](https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/) supports consistency and individualisation; training to failure is not necessary for the average healthy adult. Our conservative workload examples and progression prompts are product drafts, not a verbatim ACSM programme. We do not prescribe one starting weight for all users or force increases from attendance alone.

[US Army Basic Leader Course assessment guidance, 2019, pages 46–48](https://home.army.mil/bragg/application/files/4015/7850/7246/ISAP_December_2019.pdf) includes mountain climbers, push-up variations, jacks, lunges, preparation and recovery drills. This informed the movement families. Fight Hub circuits are independently assembled and must never be described as an official military programme or military-endorsed training. Hard settings are optional; harder is not automatically better.

## Premium direction and market reference

[Nike Training Club](https://www.nike.com/ntc-app) already offers broad workout and training content. [Freeletics Adapt Session](https://www.freeletics.com/en/blog/posts/quick-adapt/) already adjusts sessions for constraints including time, equipment, space, noise and difficulty. Breadth and an interval timer alone are insufficient differentiation.

Next priorities are reviewed multi-week strength and conditioning programmes; available-equipment and readiness matching; session adaptation with clear explanations; comparable logged performance; and combat-sport interests connected to the existing Fight Hub content. Retention should reward consistency and recovery, not daily maximal effort. Keep basic movement instructions and easier alternatives available free. Paid value should come from useful planning, progression and insight.

Production work still required: professional technique/programme review, image corrections where anatomy or sequencing is unclear, account-backed history, secure subscription enforcement, accessible video demonstrations, and field testing with beginners. No claim of outperforming competitors has been validated.
