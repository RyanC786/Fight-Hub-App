# Martial arts mobility and membership — 27 September 2026

## Implemented prototype

The Martial arts mobility screen offers three free selections: movement control, front-split foundations and middle-split foundations. Each opens the existing builder and logging flow, including preparation and cooldown. New hip-flexor, butterfly and half-split entries have original instructions, easier options and realistic AI illustrations. The catalogue now has 85 entries and 84 images; seated leg curl remains withheld for correction.

These are general physical-preparation drafts, not martial-arts instruction, validated split programmes or personalised prescriptions. Front/middle foundations do not guide a member into a full split or promise a time to achieve one. The movement-control selection precedes rather than replaces sport-specific class preparation. All content requires professional review before a paid launch.

## Proposed membership boundary

| Free | Proposed Premium |
| --- | --- |
| Every movement's instructions, image and easier option | Reviewed multi-week strength, conditioning and flexibility programmes |
| Home sessions, HIIT and three mobility foundations | Scheduling around classes, sparring and recovery |
| Custom builder, favourites and basic local logs | Session adaptation for time, equipment and member feedback |
| Basic equipment/focus matching and dated week | Comparable progress trends and controlled-range assessments |
| Preparation and cooldown guidance | Account-backed history across devices |

The current premium demo only unlocks extra gym/dumbbell/conditioning selections. Future capabilities are labelled unbuilt. There is no checkout, recurring charge or enforced subscription. Existing free capabilities remain free in this prototype; pricing is undecided. Do not imply that paying makes a stretch more suitable or that a disclaimer substitutes for content review.

## Priority features to test

1. **Class-aware weekly planning.** A member enters Tuesday/Thursday classes and Saturday sparring; the planner explains where it proposes strength, mobility and recovery. Test whether this reduces conflicts and helps people follow their plan. This is the strongest product hypothesis, not a claim of market exclusivity.
2. **Explainable session changes.** Offer a shorter session, a quieter alternative or an easier day, and explain what changed. Member-reported fatigue is input, not an injury diagnosis or readiness guarantee.
3. **Controlled mobility progress.** Consistent setup and separate left/right observations, recording comfort and control as well as range. No rewards for pain, maximum stretch depth or forced daily progression. Avoid camera-based injury or technique claims without validation.
4. **One training-and-sport calendar.** Connect chosen classes and personal training to followed Fight Hub events after the existing website integration is understood. Keep editorial data sources and permissions explicit.

First build a class-aware planner prototype and test with members; then commission programme review and validate adaptation rules. Avoid adding more paywalled exercise lists as the main justification for subscriptions. Basic movement guidance should remain available even after a member cancels.

## Sources and scope

[AAOS safe-exercise guidance](https://www.orthoinfo.org/staying-healthy/safe-exercise/) supports warming up before stretching, maintaining control and avoiding painful or bouncing stretches. Our exercise wording and assembled selections are original; AAOS does not endorse them.

[ACE mobility versus flexibility](https://www.acefitness.org/continuing-education/certified/may-2026/9119/mobility-vs-flexibility-why-the-difference-matters-for-movement-and-performance/) distinguishes available range from strength and control within that range. This informs combining gentle stretching with controlled movement; it does not validate a splits curriculum.

New exact image prompts and repository asset paths are in [MOBILITY-IMAGE-PROMPTS.json](MOBILITY-IMAGE-PROMPTS.json). Images use the built-in ChatGPT image tool and require professional technique review.
