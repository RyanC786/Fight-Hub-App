## Prompt used

Panel 1: anatomical LEFT knee lifted outward at body height in a tight roundhouse chamber, lower leg folded back, RIGHT support foot pivoting. Panel 2: same LEFT lower leg snaps outward to body height, instep as contact surface, knee slightly bent; supporting foot pivoted. Panel 3: SAME LEFT leg immediately retracts to the identical tight chamber from panel 1 without putting foot down, ready to kick again. Add small arrows chamber → kick → rechamber. Keep torso upright and fists guarding. Avoid side-kick heel presentation and avoid changing legs.
